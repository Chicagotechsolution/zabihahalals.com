Zabiha, verified. Not just "halal-friendly."

Plenty of menus say halal. Far fewer can tell you where the meat was slaughtered, by whom, and under which certification. Zabiha Halals is a city-by-city guide that lists only restaurants, butchers, and groceries serving hand-slaughtered zabiha — and tells you how each one sources it.

What counts as zabiha here

Every listing answers the questions you'd ask the owner.

Before a spot appears in this guide, we record how it answers four sourcing questions — the same ones a careful eater asks at the counter. The answers are published on the listing, so you can apply your own standard, whether you follow hand-slaughtered only, a specific certifier, or your local imam's guidance.

We don't sell placement. A listing can't pay to appear zabiha; it either documents its sourcing or it isn't listed.

  • Source of meat Which supplier or farm, and whether beef, chicken, lamb, and goat differ.
  • Method Hand-slaughtered (zabiha) or machine — stated per protein, not per menu.
  • Certification Certifier name and document date, when one exists.
  • Kitchen handling Whether non-zabiha items share fryers, grills, or prep areas.
